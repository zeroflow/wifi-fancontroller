# Glossary

The glossary fixes one preferred term per concept in English and German. It is the human version of the term rules. Every row is a proposal until the maintainer approves it.

## How entries work

- **One term per concept.** Each row names one concept and one preferred term per language. The avoid columns list the forms that the term rules flag.
- **One form per role.** For rows whose concept starts with `Role:`, the preferred cell fixes the single form that every page uses for that role (decision D-13).
- **Concept prefixes.** A concept starts with `Brand:` when it is a brand or product name, and with `Role:` when it names a person in a role.
- **Status.** One of PROPOSED, APPROVED, REJECTED or LOCKED. PROPOSED rows wait for the maintainer. LOCKED rows are fixed by a decision, such as D-05 for units.
- **Enforcement.** The rules `Terms`, `Brands` and `RoleNouns` in both languages are written only from APPROVED rows (and from LOCKED rows). A PROPOSED or REJECTED row never becomes a lint rule.
- **Counts.** The Notes column holds usage counts over `site/src/content/docs`. The counts cover prose only. Code spans, fenced blocks, link targets and frontmatter are removed first. They show how much a row would change, not how right it is.
- **Empty cells** contain the word "none".
